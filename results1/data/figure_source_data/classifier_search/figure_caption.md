**Extended Data Fig. 2 | Equal-budget hyperparameter searches using grouped internal cross-validation.** Orange, blue and green diamonds denote the five validation-fold scores of the best candidate selected by grid search, random search and Bayesian optimization (Optuna TPE), respectively. Each strategy evaluates 12 candidates on the same five folds of 964 unique previously labelled texts in 95 reporting-account/text-connected groups. Every candidate/fold starts from fresh public pretrained DistilBERT weights; neither fine-tuned weights nor checkpoints cross folds. Learning rates range from 10⁻⁵ to 5×10⁻⁵, training lasts 3 or 5 epochs, and batch size is 8 or 16. The selection objective is the unweighted mean fold micro-F1. Loss is mean four-label binary cross-entropy; accuracy is exact-match subset accuracy; precision, recall and F1 use micro averaging; ROC AUC is the mean of four label-specific AUCs. Classification uses sigmoid probability >0.5. Grey marks show the fold mean and Student-t 95% interval. Training folds overlap, and the same fold scores selected the candidate: these intervals describe fold variation and the scores are selection-optimistic internal CV, not nested or recent-year external validation. The fixed-parameter model used to generate all current regression labels is unchanged (10⁻⁵ learning rate, 5 epochs, batch size 8); its fixed-parameter grouped-CV reference is provided separately in Source Data. No new search winner is silently substituted into the policy measures. One seeded run per candidate is compared; this does not establish a general ranking of search algorithms. Source Data include all 36 candidates, 180 fitted folds and selected OOF predictions.
